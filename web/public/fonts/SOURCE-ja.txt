ja-shinonome16m.bin  Shinonome Mincho 16 (16 x 16): the Modern look's story pages.
                     The Shinonome fonts (shinonome-0.9.11), The Electronic Font Open Laboratory:
                     public domain. http://openlab.ring.gr.jp/efont/shinonome/

Format: "U5JF", the count (u16), the side (u8), the bytes to a glyph (u8), the letters' Unicode codes (u16 each,
in order), then the glyphs, row by row from the top, the leftmost pixel the high bit. Read by src/i18n/jfont.ts.
